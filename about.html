Company story

A focused platform for one of the most visible parts of a home.

Walk-In Tub planning affects comfort, safer access, routine ease, and the daily feel of a bathroom. Yet the first step of finding provider options can feel scattered: different categories, different terminology, different quote processes, and different levels of detail.

StepEase was created to make that first step feel more organized. Instead of positioning itself as a contractor, the platform helps homeowners choose a Walk-In Tub service category, share project basics, and request contact from independent local providers.

The goal is practical clarity: homeowners should be able to compare provider responses, ask better questions, and move forward only after reviewing details directly with the companies they are considering.
